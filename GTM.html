The Problem

Small business owners — plumbers, electricians, landscapers, salons, auto shops — miss 30-60% of inbound calls because they're on a job, driving, or with another customer.

Every missed call is a lost lead. That customer calls the next business on Google. There's no second chance.

Most owners know this. They feel the pain every day. But the solutions they've seen require porting their phone number, learning new software, or paying $200-500/month.

The Swoop Solution

When a business misses a call, Swoop instantly texts the caller back:

"Hey! Mike's Plumbing here — saw we missed your call. How can we help?"

The customer replies by text. The lead is captured. The conversation starts. The job is saved.

The Zero-Risk Setup

"Keep your number. Change nothing. Dial one code."

This is Swoop's #1 competitive advantage.

Business owners DON'T need to:

  • Change their phone number

How It Works (60-Second Explanation)

  1. You keep your existing business number — the one on your truck, your cards, your Google listing
  2. You dial a short code on your phone (we'll walk you through it — takes 10 seconds)
  3. When a customer calls and you pick up — nothing changes, normal call
  4. When you miss a call — Swoop catches it and texts the customer back instantly
  5. You see every missed call and conversation on your Swoop dashboard

To turn it off? Dial one code. Done. No cancellation calls, no hoops.
