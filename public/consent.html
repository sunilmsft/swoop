Swoop. SMS Consent & Opt-In Policy

What is Swoop?

Swoop is an automated missed-call text-back service for small home-service businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, landscapers, cleaners, and more). When a customer calls a business and the call is missed or goes unanswered, Swoop sends a text message to the caller acknowledging the missed call and offering assistance.

How Customers Opt In

Customers provide implied consent by initiating an inbound phone call to the business. No pre-registration, sign-up form, or marketing list is involved. The message flow is:

1

Customer calls the business phone number.

2

The call is missed or goes unanswered.

3

Swoop sends a single SMS acknowledging the missed call and offering to help (e.g., "Hey! Sorry we missed your call. What do you need help with?").

4

If the customer replies, an AI assistant answers questions about the business's services, pricing, and availability.

5

The conversation is handed off to the business owner if it requires human attention.

Message Types

  • Missed-call acknowledgment — Sent once after a missed call
  • Follow-up reminders — Up to 3 follow-ups if the customer doesn't respond
  • AI-assisted replies — Only sent in response to customer-initiated messages
  • Review requests — Sent only after a completed service, with customer consent

No marketing or promotional messages are sent. All messages are transactional responses to customer-initiated contact.

How to Opt Out

Customers can opt out at any time by replying:

  • STOP — Stops all future messages
  • HELP — Receives information about the service and how to opt out

Once a customer replies STOP, no further messages will be sent from that business's number.

Message Frequency

Customers will receive a maximum of 1 missed-call text per unanswered call, up to 3 follow-up reminders, and AI replies only when the customer initiates a conversation. Typical volume is 1–5 messages per interaction.
