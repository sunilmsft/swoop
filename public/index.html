⚡ Quick Setup

Swoop is running! Here's how to connect it to Twilio:

  1. Copy .env.example to .env and add your Twilio credentials
  2. Buy a Twilio phone number ($1/mo + usage)
  3. Set the Voice webhook to YOUR_URL/webhooks/voice
  4. Set the Voice status callback to YOUR_URL/webhooks/voice-status
  5. Set the SMS webhook to YOUR_URL/webhooks/sms
  6. Register your business below 👇
