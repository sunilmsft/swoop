Swoop. SMS Consent & Opt-In Policy

What is Swoop?

Swoop is an automated missed-call text-back service for small home-service businesses (plumbers, electricians, HVAC, landscapers, cleaners, and more). SMS messages are sent only after verbal consent is collected by the business.

How Customers Opt In

Customers opt in verbally during a phone conversation with the business before SMS messages are sent.

Verbal consent script used by the business:

"Thanks for calling [Business Name]. We can send service-related text messages about your inquiry, estimate, appointment, or follow-up at this phone number. Message and data rates may apply. You can reply STOP at any time to opt out, or HELP for help. Do you agree to receive these text messages?"

Customer response required:

The customer must verbally say "Yes" before SMS messages are sent.

If the customer says no:

No SMS messages are sent.

How consent is recorded:

The business records the customer's phone number, date/time of verbal consent, and the service inquiry context.

Message Types

  • Missed-call acknowledgment - Sent once after a missed call
  • Follow-up reminders - Up to 3 follow-ups if the customer doesn't respond
  • AI-assisted replies - Only sent in response to customer-initiated messages
  • Review requests - Sent only after a completed service, with customer consent

No marketing or promotional messages are sent. All messages are transactional responses to customer-initiated contact.

How to Opt Out

Customers can opt out at any time by replying:

  • STOP - Stops all future messages
  • HELP - Receives information about the service and how to opt out

Once a customer replies STOP, no further messages will be sent from that business's number.

Message Frequency

Customers will receive a maximum of 1 missed-call text per unanswered call, up to 3 follow-up reminders, and AI replies only when the customer initiates a conversation. Typical volume is 1-5 messages per interaction.
