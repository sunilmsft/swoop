# Trademark / Naming — Questions to Ask a Lawyer

Tracked separately from the main workback plan. Bring this list to the first consultation.

## Context to give upfront

- Product: AI-assisted missed-call text-back for small home-service businesses (plumbers, HVAC, electricians, cleaners)
- Working name: "Swoop"
- Found via informal search (not a legal opinion): a competing product called "Swoopd" doing nearly the same thing for tradies in NZ/AU, and multiple active US trademark registrations for "SWOOP" in software/SaaS classes, including one held by SwoopMe Inc. for "software for mobile phones... for managing services"
- Business entity: WelcomeMat Digital LLC (Washington state), no separate entity for this product yet

## Core questions

1. Given what we found, how real is the conflict risk with the existing "SWOOP" trademark registrations — specifically the SwoopMe Inc. mark, since it's in a software/services class close to ours?
2. Does "Swoopd" being a real, active competitor with a near-identical name change your recommendation, even though they're not a registered US trademark holder (if that's the case) and operate in a different market (NZ/AU)?
3. Would you recommend abandoning "Swoop" entirely, or is there a defensible variation (different spelling, added word, different visual mark) that reduces the risk enough to proceed?
4. If we proceed with "Swoop" anyway, what's the realistic worst case — a cease-and-desist, an opposition during registration, or something more costly?
5. Can we register "Swoop" as a Washington state trade name (DBA) under WelcomeMat Digital LLC even if federal trademark registration is risky or denied? Are those two things independent?
6. For our A2P 10DLC brand registration with Twilio — does the brand name need to exactly match the LLC's legal name, or can it be a registered DBA? (Worth confirming with Twilio directly too, but want your read on it.)
7. What's your actual flat-fee quote for: a full clearance search, an opinion on our specific risk, and — if you recommend proceeding — the federal filing itself?
8. Roughly what's the timeline — how long before we'd know if there's a problem, versus how long full registration takes?
