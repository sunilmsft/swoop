What is Swoop?

Swoop is an AI-powered missed-call text-back service for small businesses.

A plumber is under a sink. Phone rings. Can't answer. Customer calls a competitor. That's $300 gone — and it happens 3–5 times a week.

Swoop fixes this: Missed call → instant text in 3 seconds → AI conversation → lead captured → follow-up sequence → Google review request. All automated.

Voicemail Detection

When an owner declines a call or doesn't pick up, their carrier voicemail may answer. Twilio reports this as DialCallStatus: completed. Swoop detects it by checking DialCallDuration < 15 seconds — real conversations are longer, voicemail pickups are typically < 10 seconds.

Twilio Setup Reference

Twilio Console: console.twilio.com

Account Setup

  1. Create a Twilio account at twilio.com/try-twilio
  2. Get your Account SID and Auth Token from the dashboard
  3. Buy a phone number (toll-free recommended for SMS deliverability)
  4. Add to .env: 
    TWILIO_ACCOUNT_SID=ACxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
TWILIO_AUTH_TOKEN=xxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxxx
TWILIO_PHONE_NUMBER=+1xxxxxxxxxx

Webhook Configuration

In the Twilio console, configure your phone number's webhooks:

SettingValue
Voice — A Call Comes Inhttps://your-domain.com/webhooks/voice (HTTP POST)
Voice — Call Status Changeshttps://your-domain.com/webhooks/voice-status (HTTP POST)
Messaging — A Message Comes Inhttps://your-domain.com/webhooks/sms (HTTP POST)

Dev Tunnels (Local Development)

Twilio needs a public URL to send webhooks. Use VS Code Dev Tunnels:

  1. Open VS Code → Ports panel → Forward port 3000
  2. Set visibility to Public
  3. Copy the tunnel URL (e.g., https://xxxxx-3000.usw2.devtunnels.ms)
  4. Update Twilio webhook URLs to use this tunnel URL
Note: The tunnel URL changes each session. Update Twilio webhooks when it changes.

Toll-Free Verification

Toll-free numbers require verification before SMS messages will deliver. Submit verification in the Twilio console under Messaging → Toll-Free Verification. Approval can take 1-5 business days.

Voice calls work immediately — no verification needed for calling.

AI Reply Agent Design

Overview

The AI agent handles the first 3-4 text message turns with a new lead. Its job is to acknowledge, qualify, and hand off — not to close the deal or be the business owner.

Turn-by-Turn Strategy

TurnAI's JobExample
1 Warm greeting + open question "Hey! Mike's Plumbing here — saw we missed your call. What do you need help with?"
2 Acknowledge need + qualifying question "Got it — a leaky kitchen faucet. Are you in the Seattle area? And is this something you'd want fixed this week?"
3 Confirm + set expectation "Perfect, we can definitely help with that. I'll have Mike reach out to you within 2 hours to get this scheduled."
4 (if needed) Answer one follow-up + same handoff "Great question — yes, we offer free estimates for jobs over $200. Mike will have all the details when he calls."

Graceful Handoff

After the turn limit, the AI sends a handoff message and notifies the business owner.

What the customer sees:

"Thanks for the details, John! I've passed this along to Mike — he'll personally reach out to you within 2 hours. If it's urgent, feel free to call back."

What the business owner sees (dashboard + notification):

New Lead — Needs Attention
John (+14255551234) — Kitchen faucet leak, Seattle area, wants it fixed this week.
3 messages exchanged. Customer expecting callback within 2 hours.

Configurable timeframes:

ScenarioHandoff Message
During business hours"…within 1-2 hours"
After hours"…first thing tomorrow morning"
Emergency services"…within 30 minutes"

Grounding: System Prompt Structure

The business knowledge base is assembled into a system prompt for the LLM:

You are a friendly text-message assistant for {business_name}.
Owner: {owner_name}. 

ABOUT THE BUSINESS:
{description}

SERVICES: {services_list}
PRICING: {pricing_info}
SERVICE AREA: {service_area}
HOURS: {hours}

RULES:
- Keep responses short (1-3 sentences, SMS-friendly)
- Never quote exact prices unless specified in the pricing info
- After 3 turns, hand off to the business owner
- Tone: {tone_setting}
- NEVER: {never_say_rules}

YOUR GOAL: Acknowledge the customer's need, ask one qualifying 
question, then hand off to {owner_name} with a callback timeframe.
Why 3-4 turns? Enough to capture lead info and build trust. Not enough to make promises the business can't keep. The business owner gets a warm lead with context, not a cold callback.

The Problem

Small business owners — plumbers, electricians, landscapers, salons, auto shops — miss 30-60% of inbound calls because they're on a job, driving, or with another customer.

Every missed call is a lost lead. That customer calls the next business on Google. There's no second chance.

Most owners know this. They feel the pain every day. But the solutions they've seen require porting their phone number, learning new software, or paying $200-500/month.

The Swoop Solution

When a business misses a call, Swoop instantly texts the caller back:

"Hey! Mike's Plumbing here — saw we missed your call. How can we help?"

The customer replies by text. Our AI agent qualifies the lead in 2-3 turns, then hands off to the owner with a summary. The job is saved.

🧠 Solo Founder Notes

Advice to yourself. Read this when motivation dips.

On Finding a Co-Founder

  • Nobody will care as much as you. That's not a bug — you have skin in the game, they don't. Yet.
  • Interest without action is the default. Most people say "that's cool" and do nothing. The rare ones who act show up after traction, not before.
  • A partner who matches your energy usually appears after you have proof — a paying customer, a screenshot, a number. Right now you're selling a vision. After 5 customers, you're selling results.
  • Solo means: fast decisions, 100% equity, no convincing. That's an advantage.

On Outreach Fear

  • You don't need a co-founder to solve outreach. You need a system. The scripts above ARE the system.
  • Your demo is more visceral than 99% of SaaS — their phone literally buzzes in their hand. Most salespeople would kill for that.
  • Your first 5 customers won't come from a machine. They'll come from you, awkwardly texting a business owner from Google Maps. That's how every SaaS starts.
  • Cheaper alternatives to a sales co-founder: Fiverr cold callers ($5-15/hr using your scripts), BNI/Chamber of Commerce meetups (free), one happy customer telling one friend (free).

The Only Goal That Matters Right Now

3 live demos this week.

Find 10 businesses on Google Maps (mix trades — not just plumbers). Text them Script A. If 3 respond, demo for them. If 1 signs up — you have traction, proof, and a story. That changes everything.

Deployment (Render)

The project includes a render.yaml for one-click Render deployment.

  1. Push to GitHub
  2. Connect repo to Render
  3. Set environment variables in Render dashboard
  4. Update Twilio webhook URLs to your Render URL
  5. Set NODE_ENV=production on Render
SQLite caveat: Render's free tier has ephemeral disk — DB resets on redeploy. For production, migrate to PostgreSQL or use a persistent disk ($7/mo).
