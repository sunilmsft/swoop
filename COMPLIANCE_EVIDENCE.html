Submitted Copy (Exact Text — May 26, 2026 submission)

Use Case Description

This number is used by a local service business for customer-care texts after a customer contacts the business and gives consent. Messages are for service-request follow-up, appointments, estimates, missed-call follow-up, and support related to the customer's own inquiry. No marketing, cold outreach, purchased lists, or third-party lead generation. Customers can reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help.

Sample Message

Hi [Customer Name], this is [Business Name]. Following up on your service request. What day works best for a quick call? Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg&data rates may apply.

Additional Information (Verbal Consent Script)

Verbal consent is collected by phone before SMS is sent. Business says: "We can send service-related texts about your inquiry, estimate, appointment, or follow-up. Msg&data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out or HELP for help. Do you agree?" Customer must verbally say "Yes." If no, no SMS is sent. Consent record includes phone number, date/time, and inquiry context. Customer care only; no marketing or cold outreach.

Opt-In Confirmation Message

You are opted in to receive service-related text messages from [Business Name] about your inquiry, estimate, appointment, or follow-up. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help. Msg&data rates may apply.

Help Message Sample

[Business Name]: Reply HELP for help or call [business phone]. Reply STOP to opt out. Msg&data rates may apply.
