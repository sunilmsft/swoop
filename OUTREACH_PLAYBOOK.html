Core Positioning

  • Problem: Missed calls turn into lost jobs.
  • Outcome: Customer still gets a text, owner gets lead details for follow-up.
  • Risk removal: Early tester pilot, no cost, no commitment, easy to pause.
Personalization Tokens
  • [FirstName] - person name
  • [Trade] - business type (plumbing, HVAC, electrical, etc.)
  • [Issue] - pain they mentioned (missed calls, busy line, after-hours)
  • [YourName] - your name
  • [OptionalContext] - where you saw them (FB post/group comment)

Quick Personalization Checklist

  1. Replace all tokens: [FirstName], [Trade], [Issue], [YourName].
  2. Keep one CTA only (do not stack asks).
  3. Keep first-touch under 4 lines.
  4. Remove anything that sounds pushy or urgent.

Plan B If Twilio Is Pending

  1. Run a concierge pilot for first prospects.
  2. Keep voice/callback capture active so no lead is lost.
  3. Parallel-evaluate alternate providers (Telnyx or Bandwidth).
  4. Keep external positioning: no-cost pilot, no commitment, easy on/off.
